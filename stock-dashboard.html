03The pre-run pattern

What the biggest winners shared 6–12 months before the move. A screening template, not a guarantee.

STEP 01

Revenue inflection

Revenue already accelerating in filings — before the story is obvious in the price.

STEP 02

Margin expansion

Operating leverage or pricing power shows up in gross and incremental margin trends.

STEP 03

Customer validation

Named hyperscaler, sovereign or defence customers; backlog growth; multi-year wins.

STEP 04

Narrative shift

Reframed from "old economy" to "strategic bottleneck" for AI, nuclear, defence, or energy transition.

06Actionable takeaways

The shortlist — what to do with this research.

i.

Best early-stage hunting ground looks like uranium and nuclear fuel/services — especially UUUU, NXE, LEU and diversified uranium exposure.

ii.

UK defence second-liners — QinetiQ, Babcock, Avon Protection — are more accessible and less crowded than the most owned US themes.

iii.

Use prior winners like SNDK, WDC, LITE, Bloom as templates for pattern recognition, not fresh entries after their re-ratings.

iv.

Hunt for filing evidence: accelerating revenue, better margins, named customers, backlog growth, and a strategic bottleneck role before mainstream saturation.

v.

Avoid the most media-saturated and retail-driven segments unless position sizing is explicitly speculative.

vi.

The pattern that worked: "revenue is already inflecting because AI / nuclear / defence customers need this bottleneck solved" — not "company mentions AI."
